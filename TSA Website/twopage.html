Ethical Use

Some ethical uses of AI consist of keeping a safe, positive, pleasing experience to people. Some of these include non-discrimination, this removes the AI to have any bias regarding race, gender, or sexuality. Privacy is used to keep personal feelings and questions private, this can create a safe haven for many people. Lastly, AI is very reliable, AI has some of the latest, up to date, answers for people and creates trust between the AI and the user.
